## 2. What TYFINO is

TYFINO is an Android media player/client with a separate application-licensing service.

TYFINO does not provide, host, sell, proxy, restream, or administer IPTV subscriptions or third-party media catalogs.

Users bring their own compatible IPTV provider access.

## 3. User responsibility for third-party content

The user is responsible for:

- using only services and content they are authorized to access;
- complying with the terms of their IPTV/content provider;
- complying with applicable copyright, intellectual-property, and other laws;
- keeping provider credentials confidential.

TYFINO does not grant rights to third-party content.

## 4. Application licensing

V1 licensing behavior is defined by the approved TYFINO licensing contracts.

Current product rules include:

- an explicit seven-day free trial, started only after the user requests it and the server accepts the request;
- paid Activation Codes for one-year or lifetime application licenses;
- one active installation per paid Activation Code in V1;
- an authorized administrator may reset an active device binding;
- a lifetime license has no time-based expiry but may still be revoked for refund, fraud, abuse, or operational correction under the approved licensing contract.

The application license is independent from the user's IPTV subscription.

## 5. Third-party IPTV services

Third-party IPTV providers are independent from TYFINO.

TYFINO is not responsible for:

- provider availability;
- channel/catalog availability;
- provider subscription status;
- provider content legality;
- provider network quality;
- provider account suspension;
- third-party terms or privacy practices.

TYFINO لا يوفّر أو يستضيف أو يبيع أو يعيد بث أو يدير اشتراكات IPTV أو مكتبات المحتوى التابعة للغير.
